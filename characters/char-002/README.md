## Visual concept

A small-bodied chibi character with a very large, wide, rounded head. The skin is vivid green, while the oversized hair is pure white with soft pale-gray shading. The rendering is flat and hand-drawn, with no outline and deliberately simple, slightly imperfect edges.

The white hair forms a large rounded dome over the skull. Long triangular bangs fall toward the face, with four pointed tufts on the left and three on the right. Keep the hair detail minimal, with only a couple of simple internal strands.

A dark red bow sits on the upper-right side of the hair. A loose blue double-loop scribble rests on the forehead area, above the bangs.

The character has two large glossy black oval eyes. Each eye has a large white highlight, two tiny white dots, and a small four-point white star. The gaze points slightly forward and downward. There are no eyebrows or eyelashes.

Both cheeks have a pale pink spiral mark, each drawn as roughly two and a half turns. The mouth is a tiny pink three-line scribble, giving a shy, embarrassed expression. Three rounded pink dots float just outside the upper-right side of the head.

The torso is short and green. Both arms are crossed in front of the chest; hands and fingers are not visible. The composition is cropped around the middle of the torso.

## Signature features
