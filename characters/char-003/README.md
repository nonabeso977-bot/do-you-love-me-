# Character 003

**Project:** Beat Orbit  
**ID:** `char-003`  
**Status:** Concept profile  
**Display name:** Not assigned yet

## Visual concept

A full-body chibi character with a large, wide, rounded face in a warm light-brown skin tone (`#C19A72`). The original reference focused on the head only; this specification adds the outfit and body while preserving the described head design.

The hair is medium-to-dark brown (`#7A5A3E)), forming a large rounded dome with asymmetrical pointed tufts. The front is divided into triangular bangs that leave both eyes visible. A prominent side lock projects from the upper-left side and is tied with a wide red ribbon placed diagonally around it. Additional pointed locks frame the sides of the face and reach approximately jaw level.

The face has two simple black rounded-rectangular eyes. The left eye is slightly larger and higher than the right. A thin curved upper-lid line sits separately above each eye; these lines are not extra eyes. There is no visible nose, eyebrows, eyelashes, ears, iris detail, or eye highlights. A small downward-curved mouth gives the character a tired, flat, unimpressed, slightly sad expression.

A tiny yellow chick sits on top of the hair. It has an egg- or pear-shaped body, two tiny black dot eyes, a small orange beak, and two small wings. It has no visible legs and does not touch the character's face.

## Outfit

- **Shirt:** red sleeveless shirt (`#C0392B`), with the shoulders bare and no straps or sleeves.
- **Shorts:** brown shorts (`#7A5A3E`) ending above the knees.
- **Boxing gloves:** a pair of deliberately oversized dark-red boxing gloves (`#6E1717`) that fully cover the hands.
- **Shoes:** simple chibi shoes combining red (`#8F3028`), dark brown (`#4A3028`), and off-white (`#F2F0E8`) panels.

## Signature features
