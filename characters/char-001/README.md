## Visual identity

A chibi character with a bright cyan palette and a simple, flat digital-art finish. The face is round and very pale cyan, with two plain black oval eyes and a small open O-shaped mouth. There are no nose, eyebrows, eyelashes, or lips.

The signature feature is a small third eye centered on the forehead. A thin black oval halo surrounds the head; its design includes a small pointed or diamond-like detail at the top and arrow details on both sides.

The hair is vivid cyan (`#00F6FF`) with lighter highlights and darker cyan shading. The character has pointed, cat-like ears and two pale blue wings. The right wing must be larger than the left.

The outfit is an oversized, long-sleeved dark green shirt.
